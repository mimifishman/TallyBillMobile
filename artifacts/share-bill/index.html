Good questions

The bits people ask about

Do my friends need the app to join a bill?

No. Share the 6-character join code and anyone can open the same bill — they can tap the items they had to assign themselves, not just view the total. No download or sign-up needed to join.

Can it translate receipts from other languages?

Yes. TallyBill reads receipts in any language — including right-to-left scripts like Hebrew — and pulls out the item names and prices for you. Tap Translate and every item is converted to your preferred language, which you can set as the default in Settings. The original text stays a tap away.

How are tax and tip divided?

They're shared in proportion to what each person ordered — so someone with a ₪20 dish pays less tax and tip than someone with a ₪60 dish. If one person wants a different tip, you can override it just for them.

Do I have to make an account?

Not to start. Guest mode lets you split a bill right away. Create an account only when you want your past bills saved to come back to later.

Why Expo Go instead of the App Store?

It's the fastest way to put the real, working app in your hands today. Download Expo Go (free), tap the Open TallyBill button, and you're running the genuine app. Native App Store and Play Store builds are on the way.
